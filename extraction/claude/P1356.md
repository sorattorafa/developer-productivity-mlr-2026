# DevEx in Action: A study of developer experience and its tangible impact

## Dados Bibliográficos
- **Autores**: Nicole Forsgren, Eirini Kalliamvakou, Abi Noda, Michaela Greiler, Brian Houck, Margaret-Anne Storey
- **Ano**: 2024
- **Venue**: Communications of the ACM
- **DOI**: 10.1145/3643140
- **Fonte**: Google Scholar
- **Paper ID**: P1356

## Objetivo
Este estudo empírico examina a experiência de desenvolvedores usando Teoria de Design de Trabalho, propondo um modelo com três dimensões: flow state, feedback loops e carga cognitiva. Através de survey com 2.213 participantes, demonstra impacto positivo em resultados individuais, de time e organizacionais. O estudo identifica que tempo para trabalho profundo e loops rápidos de feedback têm maior impacto na produtividade e criatividade.

## Métricas e Instrumentos de Avaliação
- **Frameworks mencionados**: Work Design Theory (WDT), SPACE framework
- **Métricas mencionadas**: Flow state, Feedback loops, Cognitive load, Developer outcomes (learning, job performance, creativity), Team outcomes (productivity, code quality, innovation), Organization outcomes (retention, profitability, ability to meet goals)
- **Métricas propostas**: Developer Experience Model with three dimensions (Flow state, Feedback loops, Cognitive load) and their impact on developer, team, and organizational outcomes. Proposes measurement of: satisfaction with time for deep work, interruption frequency, time to get answers, code review turnaround, ease of understanding code, self-efficacy
