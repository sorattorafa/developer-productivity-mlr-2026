## Objetivo

Propor, demonstrar e avaliar um framework prescritivo e baseado em fases para operacionalizar praticas DevOps no desenvolvimento com Microservices architecture (e, via adapters, em modular monolith, SOA e cloud/edge). O framework especifica lifecycle contracts (inputs, activities, outputs, quality gates), papeis e responsabilidades (RACI) e um modelo de metricas e evidencias baseado em DORA metrics e SLO/error budgets para governar decisoes de promotion/rollback. O artigo tambem pretende avaliar em que medida a aplicacao do framework melhora delivery performance e reliability em relacao a condicoes de baseline ou controle.

---

## Questoes de Pesquisa

- RQ1: "What socio-technical and operational conditions are consistently required to implement DevOps effectively in microservices-oriented organizations?"
- RQ2: "How can these conditions be operationalized into a coherent, phase-based framework that specifies lifecycle contracts (activities, artifacts, gates), responsibilities, and evidence requirements?"
- RQ3: "To what extent does applying the proposed framework improve delivery and reliability performance in practice (e.g., DORA metrics and SLO-based service health indicators) compared to baseline or control conditions?"

Hipoteses da avaliacao formal (Sec. 6.2.1):
- H1 (Throughput): "Adoption of the framework reduces lead time for changes and increases deployment frequency."
- H2 (Stability): "Adoption of the framework reduces the change failure rate and mean time to recovery."
- H3 (Service health): "Adoption of the framework increases SLO compliance (e.g., p95 latency, error rate) under realistic load."
- H4 (Team capability): "Adoption of the framework improves team-perceived deployment confidence, test coverage, and release process clarity."

Nota: na p. 2 o texto resume as tres RQs de outra forma ("(1) creating actionable guidelines for MS development using DevOps practices, (2) instantiating those guidelines with a concrete toolchain, and (3) understanding them as DevOps best practices tailored to MS"), diferente das RQ1–RQ3 formais.

## Frameworks e Metricas

### Frameworks Referenciados
- **DORA metrics / Accelerate** (Forsgren, Humble & Kim, 2018) — APPLIED: DF, LT, CFR e MTTR sao as medidas primarias de outcome no demonstrador (Sec. 5.3) e no quasi-experimento (Sec. 6.2–6.3), e compoem a Layer 5 do framework. O livro Accelerate e REFERENCED como origem das capabilities e dos constructos.
- **Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)** (Beyer et al., 2016) — SLIs/SLOs e error budgets — APPLIED: SLO attainment, p95 latency, error rate e error-budget burn usados como service-health outcomes; SLO gates usados para promotion/rollback.
- **CAMS (Culture, Automation, Measurement, Sharing)** — REFERENCED; estendido pelos autores para "CAMS + R" (Runtime governance) na Layer 1.
- **Design Science Research** (Peffers et al., 2007; vom Brocke et al., 2020) — REFERENCED como metodologia de pesquisa (nao e framework de medicao).
- **Continuous Software Engineering** (Fitzgerald & Stol) — REFERENCED.
- **Continuous Delivery** (Humble & Farley) — REFERENCED.
- **The DevOps Handbook** (Kim et al.) — REFERENCED.
- **Microservices patterns** (Richardson) e reviews/mapping studies de microservices (Di Francesco et al.; Waseem et al.; Knoche & Hasselbring) — REFERENCED.
- **RACI** — incorporado ao framework proposto (Layer 4); nao usado como instrumento de medicao.
- **OKRs** — REFERENCED de passagem ("next OKRs set" como output da fase Learn and Improve).
- **Interrupted Time-Series e Difference-in-Differences** (Bernal et al.; Linden; Angrist & Pischke; Lechner; Bertrand et al.) — metodos estatisticos reportados como aplicados na analise (nao sao frameworks de produtividade).
- SPACE, DevEx, GQM, HEART — Nao reportado.

### Frameworks Propostos
Framework DevOps–microservices em cinco camadas, descrito como "a prescriptive method, not just a process map" e "architecture-agnostic by Design":
- **Layer 1 — Principles and outcomes (normativa):** CAMS + R; value orientation (ligar flow metrics — lead time, deployment frequency, change-fail rate, MTTR — a business KPIs como conversion e NPS); "Evidence over opinion".
- **Layer 2 — Decision matrices and guardrails:** architecture suitability matrix (MS vs modular monolith vs SOA, por domain volatility, team autonomy needs, transactionality, latency budget, compliance); deployment topology matrix (blue/green, canary, rolling, por SLO risk class e blast radius); contract strategy (consumer-driven contracts vs schema-evolution rules; rollback ligado a error budgets).
- **Layer 3 — Phase contracts** (Inputs, Activities, Outputs, entry/exit criteria) para seis fases: Plan and Scope; Build and Verify (exit: "≥95% critical path unit/contract tests green; security scan clean"); Integrate and Package (SBOM, provenance); Release and Govern (progressive delivery com SLO/error-budget gates); Operate and Observe; Learn and Improve.
- **Layer 4 — Roles and RACI:** Product/Value Owner, Platform/DevOps, Service Teams, SRE, Security/Compliance; "service SLOs owned by service teams; gates owned by SRE/Platform; policies owned by Value Owner".
- **Layer 5 — Metrics and evidence model:** Flow (DORA 4), Quality, Reliability (SLO attainment, error-budget burn), Security (CVE burn-down), Business (cycle-time to value, KPI adoption) e um "Evidence register (machine-readable)".
- **Method of application:** ciclo de sete passos com gates (Initiate and Tailor; Model and Plan; Implement and Verify; Package and Secure; Progressive Delivery; Operate and Review; Retrospect and Evolve) e quatro maturity levels (Seed → Foundation → Scaled → Evidence-Driven).
- **Adapters:** Modular Monolith, SOA e Cloud/Edge, trocando apenas a Layer 2.
- **Toolchain de referencia:** VCS → CI → Artifact Repository → Policy Engine → Orchestrator → Telemetry; use case com ferramentas por fase (Table 5: Jira, Confluence, Microsoft Teams, OpenAPI, Docker, .NET Aspire, GitHub Actions, Kubernetes, Postman, JMeter, Prometheus, Selenium/Puppeteer, ELK, Slack etc.).
- Figure 1 liga gaps (G1–G4) a mecanismos (M1–M6) e outcomes (O1–O5: maior DF, menor LT, menor CFR, menor MTTR, melhor SLO attainment). Table 7 mapeia cada elemento do framework as DORA metrics e conceitos SRE que ele mais impacta.
- Nota de conversao: a lista de phase contracts da Layer 3 (p. 17) saiu embaralhada no texto extraido (inputs/outputs fora de ordem em relacao as fases), mas pode ser reconstruida pela Table 4; Figure 2 (fluxo com Lane A — CI pipeline gates e Lane B — environment validation & production gates) e Figure 1 chegaram so como rotulos soltos, com partes ilegiveis.

## Resultados Principais

- Validacao inicial (Sec. 6.1): 3 entrevistas semi-estruturadas com 4 experts (3, 20, 27 e 21 anos de experiencia em TI); "All four experts found the framework to be useful and precise with what is currently implemented in their companies". Sugestoes: renomear a primeira fase ("Planning Microservices Architecture"), posicionar "Integration Testing" apos "Microservices Integration" ou em ambiente de Dev; divergencia entre Expert 1 e Expert 2 sobre "Load testing" e "performance monitoring" serem fases. "Overall, no major recommendations were made".
- Demonstracao (Sec. 5.3): tres change scenarios em duas sprints — R1 (config change): lead time ~2 h, sem SLO breach, CFR 0%; R2 (code change em dois microservicos): canary violou o SLO de latencia (p95 ≈ 310 ms), rollback automatico, promocao apos caching fix, lead time ~1 day, um failed change; R3 (schema change): lead time ~1.5 days, CFR 0%.
- Quasi-experimento (Table 8; T1 + T2; 6 semanas de baseline, 3 de adocao, 12 de steady state; agregacao semanal):
  - DF: mediana 0.8 → 2.1 deploys/day (+1.3/day, +162%); ITS level +0.92/day (95% CI 0.51, 1.34; p < 0.001); DiD ATT +1.06/day (p = 0.004).
  - LT: mediana 2.8 → 0.9 days (−1.9 days, −68%); ITS level −1.7 days (95% CI −2.3, −1.1; p < 0.001); DiD ATT −1.4 days (p = 0.008).
  - CFR: 16.0% → 8.0% (−8.0 pp, −50%); ITS slope −0.45 pp/week (95% CI −0.80, −0.09; p = 0.015); DiD ATT −6.2 pp (p = 0.031).
  - MTTR: mediana 5.4 h → 1.9 h (−3.5 h, −65%); ITS level −2.7 h (95% CI −3.9, −1.4; p < 0.001); DiD ATT −2.1 h (p = 0.012).
  - SLO attainment 96.1% → 99.2% (+3.1 pp); p95 latency −21%; error rate −43%; error-budget burn −58% (so mudancas relativas; sem ITS/DiD nem p-valor).
  - Canary gates: 4 rollbacks automaticos no inicio da adocao e 1 no steady state; o controle teve 7 "comparable incidents without automatic rollback".
- H4: critical-path test coverage +18 pp (62% → 80%); PR review time −23% (mediana); deployment confidence +1.1 pontos e process clarity +0.9 pontos (5-point Likert).
- Robustness checks: placebo DiD com breakpoint 4 semanas antes "yields non-significant effects" (sem valores); excluindo as duas primeiras semanas pos-adocao os efeitos aumentam (DF ATT +1.19/day; MTTR ATT −2.4 h); excluir o servico de maior trafego preserva sinal e significancia das quatro DORA metrics; discrepancias CI/CD vs dashboards < 3%.
- Conclusao dos autores: H1, H2 e H3 "supported"; H4 "partially supported" ("longer follow-up is needed to confirm persistence"). Os autores afirmam que "Statistical robustness checks confirmed that improvements were causally linked to framework adoption, not external factors" (p. 25).

Observacao de auditoria: o texto tem inconsistencias internas relevantes para a elegibilidade. (i) O Abstract (p. 1) descreve a validacao apenas por "expert interviews" e um "theoretical use case", e as Conclusions (p. 34) falam em "expert-informed iteration and a structured plan to capture quantitative signals", enquanto a Table 1 (p. 4) e as Sec. 6.2–6.3 (p. 26–33) reportam um quasi-experimento executado; as Limitations (p. 35) dizem que "the current validation emphasizes expert-guided refinement and single-context instantiation" e que a generalizacao "requires multi-team, multi-system deployments with longitudinal measurement and statistical baselines", e o future work preve "controlled field evaluations ... with pre-registered SLO/DORA baselines". (iii) As contagens de deployments (238 no baseline de 6 semanas vs 421 pos-adocao, com 3 semanas de adocao e 12 de steady state; p. 30–31) nao indicam aumento do numero de deployments por semana, enquanto a Table 8 reporta DF mediana 0.8 → 2.1 deploys/day (+162%); a unidade de DF nao e informada. ## Desafios do Uso das Metricas

- Literatura revisada: outcomes frequentemente self-reported ("perceived improvements rather than objective operational metrics"), sem baselines longitudinais nem controles para inferencia causal.
- "Even when DORA metrics are referenced, the measurement approach and observation period are often not described in sufficient detail to support replication."
- Estudos DevOps enfatizam "technical lead-times over explicit business KPIs", dificultando atribuir valor; a medicao "often remains narrowly operational".
- Dificuldade de transformar telemetry em sinais de governanca acionaveis (promotion/rollback); organizacoes coletam telemetry sem mecanismo repetivel de decisao.
- Construct validity: "different organizations may adopt alternative thresholds/telemetry" para DORA e SLOs.
- Escolhas de definicao do proprio estudo afetam as metricas: CFR inclui rollbacks automaticos por SLO gate; LT e MTTR usam medianas para reduzir outliers; blackout periods (holidays, release freezes) excluidos.
- Controles propostos: "definition lock" das metricas antes do breakpoint de adocao; cross-checks de contagens de deployments entre CI/CD e runtime; identity resolution (pipeline run, image tag, commit/PR) para rastreabilidade.
- Restricoes de confidencialidade: dados organizacionais reportados so de forma agregada (metricas semanais e contagens anonimizadas de incidentes).

## Limitacoes

- Demonstracao: "The scenarios are representative but limited in number; effects may vary with service complexity and dependency depth" (internal validity); "small sample size and context specificity".
- External validity: demonstracao em stack baseada em Kubernetes; a magnitude das melhorias depende de toolchain, restricoes regulatorias, legacy coupling e maturidade DevOps de partida; replicacoes devem envolver mais times, setores e janelas de observacao mais longas.
- Construct validity: DORA metrics e SLOs padrao, mas outras organizacoes podem usar thresholds/telemetry diferentes.
- Conclusions: "the current validation emphasizes expert-guided refinement and single-context instantiation"; generalizacao exige deployments multi-team e multi-system com medicao longitudinal e baselines estatisticos.
- "Threats include selection bias in experts and the confounding effect of concurrent process changes on outcome metrics."
- H4: "longer follow-up is needed to confirm persistence."
- Future work: avaliacoes de campo controladas com baselines SLO/DORA pre-registrados; lente economica (cost-of-delay, incident cost curves); integracao de gates de seguranca/compliance (SBOM, policy-as-code); playbook e reference implementation publicos.

## Contribuicao Principal

O artigo propoe, via Design Science Research, um framework prescritivo em cinco camadas que consolida orientacoes dispersas de DevOps, SRE e microservices em phase contracts auditaveis (inputs, activities, outputs, gates), papeis RACI, matrizes de decisao arquitetural com adapters (modular monolith, SOA, cloud/edge) e um modelo de evidencias que usa DORA metrics e SLO/error-budget gates para decidir promotion/rollback. O framework e ilustrado com um use case .NET/Angular em GitHub Actions, Docker e Kubernetes e validado qualitativamente com 4 experts. O artigo reporta ainda um quasi-experimento ITS + DiD com dois times tratados e um controle, com grandes melhorias nas quatro DORA metrics e em SLO attainment, mas esse componente e pouco descrito e entra em conflito com o Abstract, as Conclusions e as Limitations do proprio texto. Para a MLR, a contribuicao mais solida e o uso de DORA + SLO como camada de medicao e governanca embutida no processo de entrega.
