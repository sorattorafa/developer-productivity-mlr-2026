## Resumo

**Measuring software engineering productivity has become increasingly complex** in the era of artificial intelligence, as traditional dashboards and proxy metrics fail to reflect true business value [1, 3]. Rather than breaking developer productivity itself, AI coding tools have broken the historical proxies used to track it, causing **activity metrics** and **actual business value** to pull apart [1]. As a result, standard dashboards frequently count the wrong indicators, while high-impact engineering work remains invisible [1].

To illustrate this distortion, the source cites data from **OpenAI**, where engineers who use **Codex** open approximately **70% more pull requests** than colleagues who do not, with the gap continuing to widen over time [3]. However, relying on raw activity counts can be deceptive because higher volume does not automatically translate into meaningful outcomes [1, 3].

Consequently, engineering leaders are advised to audit their measurement systems by explicitly separating metrics into **activity** and **outcome** categories [1]. By recognizing that traditional dashboards provide misleading signals, organizations can avoid over-indexing on inflated activity counts [1].

## Metricas Mencionadas

- **Pull Requests (PRs) open**: Citado no contexto da OpenAI, onde engenheiros que utilizam o **OpenAI Codex** abrem cerca de **70% mais pull requests** do que os demais [3].
- **Activity metrics vs. Outcome metrics**: Discutido como uma distincao essencial; dashboards tradicionais medem predominantemente proxies de atividade que distorcem a visao real de produtividade [1].

---

## Insights para Praticantes

- **AI distorce proxies de medicao**: Ferramentas de AI (como o **Codex**) inflam metricas tradicionais de atividade sem necessariamente garantir um aumento proporcional de valor de negocio [1, 3].
- **Separacao entre Activity e Outcome**: Lideres de engenharia devem categorizar suas metricas para evitar a dependencia de dashboards focados unicamente em volume de atividade [1].
- **Trabalho invisivel de alto valor**: As contribuicoes de engenharia de maior valor sao cada vez menos visiveis em dashboards tradicionais [1].
