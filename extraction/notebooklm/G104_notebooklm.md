## Resumo

The 2026 State of Engineering Management Report is the seventh consecutive annual industry report published by Jellyfish, drawing on survey data from over 635 CTOs, Engineering Managers, and Engineers [2]. The report presents current industry benchmarks regarding engineering productivity priorities, company growth outlooks, and the operational impact of AI tools on developer velocity [2].

Key findings indicate that 91% of respondents experienced an increase in team productivity over the preceding 12 months, while 84% identified engineering productivity as a primary management concern [2]. Furthermore, business optimism remains strong across organizations, with 82% indicating that their company's growth outlook has improved compared to the previous year [2].

The report also highlights significant performance gains linked to AI integration, showing that 64% of respondents are achieving a developer velocity increase of at least 25% with AI (up from 2025 levels) [2]. Additionally, Claude Code has become the most widely adopted AI coding tool, representing 39% of tool usage among surveyed teams [2].
