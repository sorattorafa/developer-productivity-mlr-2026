## Objetivo

This study aims to derive and validate sub-constructs and measurement items to evaluate Developer Experience (DX) perceived by developers who use a deep-learning (DL) platform, establishing a statistically validated reflective measurement model comprising cognitive, affective (emotional), and conative (behavioral) components [3, 9-11].

---

## Questoes de Pesquisa

- **RQ1**: Do the three sub-constructs of cognitive, affective, and conative factors construct developer experience toward the deep-learning platform at a statistically significant level? [12]

---

## Resultados Principais

- **Nomological Validity Confirmed**: Confirmatory Factor Analysis (CFA) verified that DX as a second-order construct is significantly explained by Cognitive (\\(\beta = 0.849\\)), Emotional (\\(\beta = 0.799\\)), and Behavioral (\\(\beta = 0.888\\)) first-order constructs at \\(p < 0.05\\) [3, 16, 25].
- **Balanced Dimensional Impact**: All three sub-constructs demonstrate high and relatively uniform correlations with DX, showing that rational/technical, affective/emotional, and behavioral/volitional factors are equally critical [16, 30, 31].
- **Excellent Model Fit**: The structural model met all fit criteria: \\(\chi^2/df = 2.432\\) (\\(\le 3.0\\)), \\(\text{NFI} = 0.885\\) (\\(\ge 0.8\\)), \\(\text{CFI} = 0.928\\) (\\(\ge 0.8\\)), \\(\text{TLI} = 0.917\\) (\\(\ge 0.9\\)), \\(\text{IFI} = 0.929\\) (\\(\ge 0.9\\)), and \\(\text{RMSEA} = 0.080\\) (\\(\le 0.08\\)) [32, 33].
- **High Scale Reliability & Validity**: Internal consistency (Cronbach's \\(\alpha \ge 0.858\\)), convergent validity (AVE \\(> 0.590\\); Composite Reliability \\(> 0.859\\)), and discriminant validity were fully established [16, 32, 34, 35].
- **Demographic Representation**: Verified across 225 IT professionals from South Korean enterprises (79.1% male, 53.8% aged 30–39, 84.4% holding university degrees) [6, 36].

---

## Desafios do Uso das Metricas

- **Lack of Standardized DX Metrics for AI Platforms**: Prior to this work, no empirically validated measurement scales existed specifically for evaluating developer interaction with Deep Learning platforms [29, 37].
- **Risk of Confounding Variables**: Factors such as individual work experience levels and specific commercial platform choices can create unmeasured variances in DX evaluations [38].
- **Mental Effort & Cognitive Friction**: High mental friction during platform interaction directly degrades cognitive satisfaction and behavioral adoption [14, 18].

## Limitacoes

- **Unconsidered Confounding Factors**: The analysis did not control for developer work experience or the specific brand/type of deep-learning platform used within participating companies [38].
- **Sample and Industry Scope**: Sample was limited to developers from South Korean IT companies (specifically S and L firms), potentially restricting generalizability across global or open-source contexts [6, 38].
- **Self-Reported Survey Instrument**: Reliance on self-reported survey responses, although statistical tests confirmed normal distribution and common method bias compliance [40].

---

## Contribuicao Principal

This paper provides the first statistically validated, reflective measurement instrument for Developer Experience (DX) in Deep Learning platform environments, empirically establishing that DX is holistically driven by cognitive evaluation, emotional perception, and behavioral intention [3, 31, 39].
