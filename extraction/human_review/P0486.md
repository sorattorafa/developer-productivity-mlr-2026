### Evidence (quotes with section/page) — required
- "Regarding developers’ feelings about work, we can analyze the
emotions involved in developer experience by mining
developers’ questions. We used 1,568,377 body of posts from
Stack Overflow related to Android, iOS and Windows to perform
comparisons among the three ecosystems regarding the
emotions: Joy, Fear, Sadness, Anger, and Disgust." — 

- Our results indicate that Sadness, Anger and Joy are the most common emotions

- We also identified which technical elements are
involved in these emotions.

- The Mining Software Repositories (MSR) area focuses on
uncovering useful information about software by extracting and
analyzing data from different software repositories [1]. MSR
approaches have been used for different goals, e.g., analyses of
contribution and developer behavior

- This work provides us with useful information about the importance of
studying emotions in repositories in order to provide support to
its participants.

- As the main research question of this study we defined the
following: “What are the most prevalent emotions in the three
main MSECOs (Android, iOS, and Windows Phone) based on the
body of technical questions in Stack Overflow?”

- Stack Overflow makes its data publicly available in XML format
licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0 license

- We pre-processed the textual content (Body) of the extracted
posts in four steps. First, we discard any code snippets that are
present in the posts (i.e., enclosed in <code> HTML tags),
because source code syntax (e.g., if statements and for loops)
introduces noise into the analysis phase. Next, we remove all
HTML tags (e.g., <p> and <a href="...">), since they are not the
focus of our analysis. Third, we remove common English-
language stop words such as “a”, “the” and “is”, which do not
help to create meaningful topics.

- AlchemyLanguage is a collection of APIs that offers text analysis
through natural language processing. It detects anger, disgust,
fear, joy, and sadness implied in English text. Emotion Analysis
can detect emotions that are associated with targeted phrases,
entities, or keywords, or it can analyze the overall emotional
tone of the content.

- However, it is still necessary to investigate how the emotions
of the developers change during the development phases of a
mobile application. In this paper, we analyzed only the
questions, a future work may involve an analysis of emotions
from the networks of interaction between those who question
and those who respond. Another future work will be to
investigate how emotions can attract or repel developers to or
from the MSECO. The evidence extracted using the Alchemy
Language API can be validated as future work.
