- "This paper explores the integration of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) across the Agile Scrum methodology to automate these traditionally labor-intensive phases. It proposes a structured framework that evolved from a basic messaging prototype into a comprehensive web application, utilizing multi-agent collaborative systems like MetaGPT." — PDF p. 1 (Abstract) [Research Method, AI Discussed]
- "To ensure rigorous validation of the proposed framework, the experimental design utilized a controlled, multidomain testbed simulating standard two-week Agile sprints." — PDF p. 3 [Context, Agile/Process]
- "The validation methodology employed a comparative A/B testing approach. A manual development baseline was established using historical DORA metrics from traditional engineering cohorts. The AI-assisted framework was subsequently evaluated across around 30 distinct code generation and refactoring tasks (around 15 per domain)." — PDF p. 3 [Research Method, Context, DORA Applied]
- "Furthermore, an ablation study was conducted-selectively bypassing the architectural design and defect analysis modules-to empirically validate the necessity of the complete multi-agent pipeline." — PDF p. 3 [Research Method]
- "Consequently, several Large Language Models (LLMs) were tested across the framework: premium flagship models (GPT4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini 1.5 Pro), budget-tier APIs (Gemini Flash, GPT-3.5 Turbo), and free, locally hosted opensource options via Ollama (Llama 3 8B, Mistral)." — PDF p. 3 [Research Method, Context]
- "To objectively measure the framework's success, industrystandard DORA (DevOps Research and Assessment) metrics are utilized." — PDF p. 2 [DORA Applied]
- "The overall performance and operational impact were quantified using standard DORA metrics alongside Defect Density per KLOC." — PDF p. 3 [Quality, DORA Applied]
- "While the free, local models (Llama 3, Mistral) successfully improved deployment frequency and reduced the defect rate below the traditional baseline, their propensity for logic errors required heavier human intervention during the QA phase, slightly bottlenecking the Lead Time for Changes." — PDF p. 3 [Delivery Performance, Quality]
