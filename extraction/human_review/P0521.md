### Evidence (quotes with section/page) — required
- "The term Developer eXperience (DX) emerged from the con-
cept of User eXperience (UX) [3]. ISO/IEC defines UX as “person’s
perceptions and responses resulting from the use and/or anticipated
use of a product, system or service" — 

- "This paper aims to examine the DX of
software professionals with virtual kanban boards. We conducted
focus group sessions with 10 developers to understand the issues
they faced when using these tools"

- "In our analysis, we explored the
data gathered from the perspectives of how the developers perceive
the resources of the virtual kanban tools, feel about their work, and
see the value of their contribution"

- "In our planning, the focus group session was divided into two
rounds. In the first, the preparation round, we introduced the ses-
sion steps to the participants. Then the participants read Informed
Consent Form (i) and, if terms were accepted, they signed it, individ-
ually. The participants thus filled a profile questionnaire (ii). After
that, a warm-up (iii) was conducted to introduce the participants
to the study from a practical perspective. In the warm-up (iii), we
asked the participants to draw in a blank paper the kanban board
they used in their daily work. In the execution round, the discus-
sion in group (iv) was carried out. We prepared a set of artifacts to
support the two rounds as described as follows."
