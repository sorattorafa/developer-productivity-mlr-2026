- Clarity of research objectives: 5 — Explicit aim (p. 2): perceived AI impact across the SPACE dimensions; guiding questions on productivity, dimensions and team adoption (p. 4).
- Methodological adequacy: 4 — SPACE-structured survey plus interviews and observation; analysis descriptive only, qualitative coding not described, 'focus groups' (p. 2) never reported.
- Description of sample and context: 4 — 530 of 3,500 (15%); Microsoft randomized U.S. IC pool with exclusions, externals via networks (pp. 2-3); no demographics; qualitative samples undated.
- Discussion of threats to validity: 4 — 'Survey Limitations' (p. 3): response bias, perceptions not outcomes, AI-user skew, geographic restriction; non-significance/causality caveat (p. 7); short.
- Sufficiency of reported evidence: 3 — Percentages and means only; Table 2 group n, base rates for 'seven times' and tests missing; multi-agent findings from an unreferenced study.
- Contribution to practice or theory: 4 — Useful SPACE lens on AI perceptions with team/organizational moderators and recommendations; perception-only, largely confirms prior findings.
