### Evidence (quotes with section/page) — required
- "This study investigates how
software developers interact with Generative AI (GenAI) tools in
a large-scale public sector organization" — 

- "Using a mixed-methods
approach, we analyze 13 interviews and a survey with 114 respon-
dents to explore the impact of GitHub Copilot (IDE-integrated AI)
and ChatGPT (conversational AI assistant) on software engineering
workflows"

- "Our find-
ings reveal that compatibility within existing workflows plays a
crucial role in effectively utilizing GenAI tools. We explore individ-
ual, technological, and social factors that influence the adoption of
these tools, highlighting the importance of organizational support
and collective user patterns within teams."

- "By studying both users
and non-users, we provide insights into the factors driving GenAI
adoption and the barriers preventing full integration. Our findings
contribute to discussions on AI-enhanced IDEs, human-AI collabo-
ration in coding, and the evolving role of LLM-based assistants in
modern software development."

- "In October 2023, a large public sector company, which we refer
to as GovTech, introduced a pilot project aiming to see the effects
using Copilot would have on the 100 software developers who vol-
unteered to try out the business licenses available"

- "GovTech is a
large public sector organization responsible for social welfare and
labor services. With several thousand employees and servicing large
parts of the Norwegian population at different life stages, GovTech
is a prime example of a large, complex organization. We decided
to conduct a case study, as these are particularly well-suited for
delving deep into contemporary phenomena or cases in their natu-
ral settings, especially when the distinction between context and
phenomenon is unclear [26]."

- "Thirteen developers were interviewed between November 7th
and December 18th, 2023, and the interviews lasted between 30
and 60 minutes, with an average duration of 47 minutes. All the
interviews were transcribed using an AI transcription tool, but
we manually fixed the transcriptions while listening to the record-
ings, correcting mistakes and improving quality. We utilized the
Constant Comparison Method [16] for coding and memoing, and
to structure the codes into higher-level themes, we adhered to a pragmatic approach to thematic analysis [15]. First, we noted over-
all impressions, and then we reduced, organized, and coded the
data, using a reflective analysis [10]. Further, we found themes and
patterns, and we also coded following the categories described in
Russo [13], such as ”personal innovativeness" and ”social norms.”


- "The 114 survey respondents were both users (67) and non-users
(47) of Copilot in GovTech. Among the respondents, 81% were in-
house employees and 19% were external consultants (15% women,
79% men, 1% identified as "other," and 5% declined to disclose their
gender). The survey was developed based on GitHub Research’s
survey [28], which employed the SPACE framework [7] to evaluate
how participants who used a technical preview of Copilot perceived
their own productivity."
