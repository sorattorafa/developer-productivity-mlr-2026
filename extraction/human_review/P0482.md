- "To answer RQ1, the interviews revealed barriers to SMEs using low-
and no-code tools, such as training time. Beyond this, a particular
concern was expressed that low-code tools might offer too few
functions or insufficient flexibility. Only two out of the six surveyed
companies used low- or no-code platforms. One of these companies

- ". The first result stems from a survey we performed with
