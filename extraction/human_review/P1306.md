### Evidence (quotes with section/page) — required
- "In order for software development organizations and open-source
communities to improve the productivity of their software projects,
they need to intervene in factors which can actually influence pro-
ductivity in their projects. The classification adopted in this SLR
indicates where the intervention should occur: in people, prod-
uct or in the organization itself. Intervening in the organization
itself, through methods, processes and tools is much simpler than
intervening in people. This may explain a large number of organiza-
tional factors (14 factors) researched. However, it is the people who
perform the software development process and, therefore, ignor-
ing the human factors may explain the dissatisfaction with some
development methodologies: they do not consider real organiza-
tions. Therefore, it is important for organizations to balance their
productivity improvement actions by considering a combination of
human and organizational factors that are compatible with their
organizational context." —
