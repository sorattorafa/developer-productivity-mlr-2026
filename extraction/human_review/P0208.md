### Evidence (quotes with section/page) — required
- "The research highlights the role of domain expertise in
improving decision-making, reducing onboarding time,
lowering employee turnover, and fostering better
communication within development teams."

- "A mathematical
model is proposed to quantify the effects of industry knowledge
on key performance indicators (KPIs) such as development
speed, defect rate, and product-market fit."

- "The results demonstrate that integrating
industry knowledge into the software development lifecycle
significantly enhances overall efficiency and product quality"

- "To assess the differential impact across experience levels,
engineers were grouped into three categories:
• Junior engineers – individuals with limited experience
and recent onboarding;
• Middle engineers – self-sufficient professionals
capable of independent execution and mentorship;
• Senior engineers – highly experienced specialists
responsible for architectural decisions and business
alignment"


- "The survey was conducted in February 2025 by a
Ukrainian IT company as part of an internal initiative to
evaluate the impact of domain knowledge on software
engineering performance. Approximately 280 software
engineers participated, representing a broad spectrum of
technical roles (backend, frontend, QA, DevOps, and product
support) and working across diverse commercial projects in
industries such as fintech, logistics, healthcare, e-commerce,
and enterprise SaaS"


- "For each factor, engineers assigned a score from 0 (no
enhancement) to 10 (maximum enhancement), reflecting their
perception of the practical benefit of domain-specific
knowledge. The collected data were grouped according to
engineer seniority, and the average scores for each factor
within each group were calculated. These summarized results
are presented in Table 1."

- "For junior engineers, the most significant enhancement is
observed in Onboarding and Integration Speed, Outcome-
Focused Development, and Communication Efficiency. These
results suggest that domain-specific understanding helps
juniors more effectively navigate the initial phases of software
development, accelerating their adaptation and improving
coordination within teams. The highest score for onboarding
(9) emphasizes the value of clear business context and
structured induction in unlocking junior productivity.
Similarly, the elevated impact on communication and
outcome alignment points to the critical role of contextual
knowledge in facilitating clarity and purpose early in the
career."

- "Among middle-level engineers, industry knowledge most
strongly enhances Product Quality, Problem-Solving and
Decision Autonomy, and Accuracy of Estimation. These
engineers typically serve as a bridge between implementation
and planning, often mentoring juniors and ensuring delivery
quality. The high impact on autonomy (score of 8) reflects the
importance of contextual understanding in making
independent decisions, while the influence on product quality
and estimation accuracy confirms that domain expertise is a
key enabler of reliable planning and defect reduction."

- "For senior engineers, the greatest enhancements are
recorded in Relevance of Solutions, Accuracy of Estimation,
and Outcome-Focused Development. These findings are
aligned with the role of senior staff in system design,
stakeholder communication, and translating strategic
objectives into technical solutions. The maximum score in
estimation accuracy (10) demonstrates how deep domain
insight supports more realistic planning and risk management."
