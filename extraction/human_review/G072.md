- "Takeaways from Section 5 — Measure It
#3 Start a friction log
A running note, this week. Write down everything that slows you down. At week’s end,
look for patterns — then ask: which of these could AI actually help with?
#4 Time your MTWTF — once
Pick your next real alert and literally clock from page to “I understand what is
happening.” One data point is a conversation starter.
#5 Declare a toil hour
One hour, one sprint, your team logs manual and repetitive work. No fixing yet.
Visibility first."
